Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Xaver Analytics is a personal tool operated by Xaver Gry Lutz. It shows a TikTok user statistics for their own videos.

What we access

With your permission through TikTok Login, the app reads basic profile information and a list of your own videos with public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares, duration, title and publish date).

How it is used

The data is used only to display statistics to you. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens and data are kept only as long as needed to show your statistics. You can disconnect the app at any time in your TikTok account settings, and you can ask for your data to be deleted.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent via the GitHub profile hosting this page.
